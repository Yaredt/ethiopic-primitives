Privacy

Zemen Calendar · ዘመን የቀን መቁጠሪያ — zemencalendars.com and its Android app. Last updated 1 November 2026.

In short

Zemen Calendar does not collect, store or share any personal data. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What stays on your device

  • Your settings (language, numerals, theme, which holidays to show) are saved in your browser's or the app's local storage, on your device only.
  • The app saves its own files and the Bible text of the readings you open, so it works offline. You can clear them by clearing the app's storage or site data.

What the app downloads

The app downloads its files and the readings' Bible text only from zemencalendars.com. Like any website, the hosting service (GitHub Pages) may keep standard server logs, such as IP addresses, for security; Zemen Calendar does not use them.

Your date and clock

The app reads your device's clock only to open on today's date. It never sends it anywhere.

Children

The app is suitable for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

Bible text

The Amharic Bible text is © 1962, 2003 United Bible Societies, used with the permission of the Bible Society of Ethiopia for non-commercial use. The English text is the World English Bible (public domain). Zemen Calendar is free and has no ads.
