## English (en-US)

**App name** (max 30): `Zemen Calendar · ዘመን የቀን መቁጠሪያ`

**Short description** (max 80):
`Ethiopian calendar with feasts, fasts and the Orthodox daily Bible readings.`

**Full description** (max 4000):

> Zemen Calendar is the Ethiopian calendar in your pocket: all 13 months, with the Gregorian date on every day, in Amharic or English, with Ge'ez or Arabic numerals. It works fully offline.
>
> • Every holiday: national days, Ethiopian Orthodox feasts (Meskel, Genna, Timket, Fasika and the whole Easter cycle), Islamic holidays and cultural festivals.
> • Saints' days: the monthly commemoration (ወርኃዊ በዓላት) for every day of the month.
> • Fasting seasons: Abiy Tsom, Nineveh, the Apostles' fast, Filseta, the Prophets' fast, Gahad and the Wednesday/Friday fast, with "day N of M".
> • Daily Bible readings of the Ethiopian Orthodox Tewahedo Church from the መጽሐፈ ግጻዌ (lectionary): the morning and Kidase readings for every day of the year, the Sunday readings and the movable feasts. Tap a reading to read its verses in Amharic, with English a tap away.
> • A date converter between the Ethiopian and Gregorian calendars, the Evangelist year and Amete Alem.
> • Light and dark themes and a layout made for phones.
>
> The readings are a draft transcription of the lectionary and are being checked; readings still to be verified are marked "?".
>
> Free, with no ads, no accounts and no tracking. Amharic Bible text © 1962, 2003 United Bible Societies, used with the permission of the Bible Society of Ethiopia for non-commercial use; English: World English Bible (public domain).
